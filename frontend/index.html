Simple Technology for Smarter Goat Farm Management

My Goat Farms is a digital farm management platform designed to make goat farming and Palai management simple, organized and efficient.

From purchasing and registering a goat to tracking its health, weight, expenses, Palai care and final sale, you can manage the complete journey from one platform.

Our goal is simple — help farm owners reduce manual paperwork, keep accurate records and manage their business more efficiently.
