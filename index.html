Four parts of the farm, one connected record

A goat can move from trading into your own Palai, or from a customer's Palai to a sale. Its history follows it.

Customer Palai

Goats you keep and care for on behalf of customers.

  • Weight, health and services per goat
  • Monthly Palai charges and advances
  • Bills you can share with the customer

Goat trading

Buying lots, registering goats and selling them on.

  • Purchase lots and supplier payments
  • Bookings, sales and delivery
  • Profit on every lot

Own Palai

Goats your farm owns and raises.

  • Growth and feeding records
  • Treatments and breeding
  • Cost of each goat until sale

Stock & accounts

Feed, medicine and money in and out.

  • Stock levels with low-stock alerts
  • Expenses and income
  • Reports for any date range

How a goat's record builds up

Each step adds to the same record, so nothing is written twice.

  1. 1

    Arrives

    Bought in a lot or brought in by a customer. Tag, breed and starting weight are recorded.

  2. 2

    Is cared for

    Weigh-ins, vaccines, deworming, hoof cutting and treatments, with reminders when they're due.

  3. 3

    Costs are tracked

    Feed, medicine and Palai charges add up against the goat or the customer automatically.

  4. 4

    Leaves the farm

    Sold, delivered or checked out to its owner, with the bill or receipt ready to share.

What is My Goat Farms?

A mobile app for running a goat farm. It keeps one record for every goat, whether it belongs to a customer in your Palai, to your own farm, or was bought for trading, along with stock, expenses and bills. See the four modules.

What does "Palai" mean here?

Palai is when you keep and care for goats on behalf of their owners. The Customer Palai module tracks each customer's goats, their weight and health, the monthly Palai charge and any advance paid, and makes a bill you can share.

Can my partners and staff use the same farm?

Yes. The farm owner adds partners, and each partner signs in with their own account. The owner chooses what each partner can see and change.

How do I start using it?

Send us an enquiry or WhatsApp us. We'll show you the app, help you register your farm, and switch on your subscription once your farm is approved.

How much does it cost?

The app works on a subscription for your farm, with 6-month and 1-year plans. Ask us for current pricing for your farm.

Who can see my farm's records?

Only you and the partners you add. Records are stored securely in the cloud, so they aren't lost if a phone is lost or replaced. Our privacy policy explains how we handle information sent through this website.

Can I get a demo before deciding?

Yes. Choose "Book a demo" in the enquiry form and we'll arrange a time to walk you through it.
